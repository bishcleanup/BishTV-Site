Frequently Asked Questions

How does BishTV save me money?

We work directly with the major cable and satellite companies across the country. We know where the current promotions and bundle discounts are, so you get the best price available.

Do you really skip credit checks and long contracts?

Yes! We focus on easy-to-start services that do not require hard credit checks, surprise deposits, or long-term binding agreements.

What kind of home phone service is this?

We provide traditional home phone service, not internet VoIP. It includes unlimited local and long-distance calling and works like a standard landline.

Is Starlink included in the bundle?

No. Starlink is a separate, paid upgrade for customers who live in rural places and want fast satellite internet. If you want Starlink, we can help you order and set it up for an extra charge.
